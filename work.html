Thrive Collective

Website rebuild

Delivery 6 weeks

Thrive's website had been in place for more than a decade and had accumulated years of technical and content debt.

The situation

Thrive needed a public-facing digital presence that better reflected the organization it had become rather than the one that originally launched the site years earlier.

The problem

Years without consistent maintenance had made the existing site inefficient and increasingly difficult to work with.

What Brightward did

Labib led a rebuild of Thrive's website and digital presence, replacing the aging foundation with a modern implementation designed around the organization's current needs.

The result

The rebuild was completed in six weeks, replacing a site that had been operating for more than ten years.

Thrive Collective

Palette

Delivery 6 weeks

Thrive's inventory records were split across analog and digital systems, leaving information fragmented across the organization.

The situation

Different pieces of inventory information were being managed in different places rather than through one cohesive system.

The problem

The split between analog and digital recordkeeping created gaps in the workflow and made inventory harder to manage as one complete picture.

What Brightward did

Kris developed Palette, a web application designed to consolidate Thrive's inventory matters into one place.

The result

Palette was completed in six weeks and provides one digital home for work that had previously been spread across multiple forms of recordkeeping.
